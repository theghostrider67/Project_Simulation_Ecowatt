# EcoWatt: Smart Renewable Energy Storage Management System

## About The Project
EcoWatt is an intelligent, web-based centralized energy management platform designed for industrial and commercial facilities in Bangladesh. It addresses the challenges of high electricity costs, unstable power supply, and inefficient energy management systems by optimizing the coordination of solar, wind, battery storage, and grid power. 

The primary goal of EcoWatt is to reduce operational expenses during peak hours and maximize the utilization of renewable energy, thereby reducing dependence on fossil fuels and promoting sustainable industrial growth.

## Key Features
* **Smart Coordination:** Automatically integrates and manages solar, wind, battery storage, and grid electricity.
* **Cost Optimization:** Reduces electricity costs through intelligent peak and off-peak scheduling.
* **AI-Driven Forecasting:** Provides AI-based energy demand and renewable output forecasting.
* **Battery Health Management:** Monitors key parameters like State of Charge (SOC), State of Health (SOH), and temperature to ensure safe and efficient charge/discharge cycles and extend battery lifespan.
* **Sustainability Tracking:** Monitors carbon emissions and generates automated sustainability reports.
* **Real-Time Dashboard:** Offers facility managers a comprehensive web interface for continuous insights into energy flow, cost savings, and system performance.

## Target Audience
Designed primarily for industrial and commercial facilities in Bangladesh, including:
* Textile and Garment Factories
* Steel Mills
* Pharmaceutical Companies
* Food Processing Industries
* Commercial Buildings & Hotels
* Healthcare Institutions

## Development Methodology
The project follows **DSDM (Dynamic Systems Development Method)**, an Agile software development model ensuring continuous user feedback, strict adherence to time and budget constraints, and early delivery of functional business-critical components.
